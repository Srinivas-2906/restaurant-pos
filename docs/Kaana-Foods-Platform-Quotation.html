1. Executive Summary

Kaana Foods is an India-first, enterprise-grade restaurant management platform designed for multi-outlet brands, cloud kitchens, and full-service restaurants. The platform covers the complete operational lifecycle — floor service, kitchen operations, billing, inventory, procurement, human resources, payroll, reservations, analytics, and third-party aggregator integrations.
